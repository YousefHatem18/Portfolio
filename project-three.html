AI-Powered Customer Support Chatbot

A conversational support assistant built for online stores to answer product questions, handle order issues, and automate repetitive customer service tasks.

Back to portfolio

Project overview

Problem

E-commerce businesses often receive a high volume of repetitive questions about products, shipping, returns, and order details.

Solution

I created an AI chatbot that understands product and support inquiries, answers accurately, and triggers workflows through n8n to reduce workload for human agents.

Key capabilities

Product Q&A

Customers can ask about features, availability, pricing, and recommendations in natural language.

Order Support

The assistant helps with common questions related to delivery, returns, shipping status, and order status.

Workflow Automation

n8n routes complex cases to the right team while the chatbot handles routine interactions automatically.

Example

Customer conversation

The bot responds naturally to product questions and simple support requests in real time.

Support workflow

When needed, n8n forwards more complex issues to a human team while keeping routine queries automated.

Result

The chatbot improved response speed, reduced repetitive support load for staff, and created a smoother customer experience while keeping the brand responsive around the clock.
