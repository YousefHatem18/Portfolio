Project overview

Problem

Patients often reach out with vague symptoms and need quick guidance, but many clinics do not have enough time or staff to triage every message manually.

Solution

I built an AI assistant that collects symptom details, analyzes them conversationally, and routes patients toward the appropriate next steps or specialist based on urgency.

How it works

1. Intake

The assistant asks structured questions about symptoms, duration, and severity to collect key clinical context.

2. Analysis

OpenAI processes the information and generates a preliminary assessment with recommendations.

3. Routing

n8n orchestrates the workflow and can send the patient to the right department or specialist.

Example

Patient intake flow

The system asks symptom-based questions and collects patient context before triage.

Smart recommendation

AI evaluates urgency and recommends the right specialist or next step in a clear workflow.

What this project solved

Impact This workflow reduced time-to-response for patient questions, improved triage consistency, and helped clinics handle repetitive intake tasks automatically without losing a human-centered experience.
